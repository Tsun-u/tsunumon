## Requirements

### Pedagogical Structure
Use a scaffolding approach (shallow to deep):
1. **Hook** (30-60 sec): Open with an engaging question, surprising fact, or real-world scenario to grab attention.
2. **Introduction** (1-2 min): Provide background context and explain why this topic matters.
3. **Core Concepts** (5-8 min): Break down the main ideas step by step. Use analogies and visual descriptions.
4. **Worked Example** (3-5 min): Walk through a concrete example applying the concepts.
5. **Summary** (1-2 min): Recap key takeaways. End with a thought-provoking question or next steps.

### Adaptivity
- Adjust vocabulary, depth, and examples to match the student's background.
- Identify what the student likely already knows (prior knowledge) and build from there.
- Use analogies and metaphors appropriate for their age and experience level.

### Content Accuracy
- ONLY state facts you are confident about. If unsure, use web search to verify.
- For any mathematical or scientific calculations, use code execution to verify correctness.
- Do NOT fabricate data, statistics, formulas, or citations.
- Prefer well-established textbook knowledge over obscure claims.
